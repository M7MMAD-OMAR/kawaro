# S-140 Reliably cancel held Alt sessions with Escape and release
Status: implemented, independently reviewed, natively verified and installed locally; active Kawaro integration updated, public source/release pending

## Story
As a file-manager user, I want Escape or release-before-drop to cancel without the watchdog, and a fresh drag to work without restarting.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: On private Hyprland 0.57.1, Escape explicitly cancels its pending/probing/shown drop session while Alt remains held. Invalidate ownership before callbacks, clear pending files/preview/options, submit zero jobs, hand off zero times and reopen zero times for 15 s. Surface absent within 500 ms. Explicit probe cancellation must not become pointer-hide rearm.
- AC2: After another key, actual Alt-up spawns the generated release command and reaches daemon dispatch within 500 ms. Release-before-drop cancels within 500 ms, with zero jobs/handoffs/reopens and no `release-lost`. Escape must work before Alt-up independently. Non-Escape keys after show do not themselves cancel; pre-threshold chord suppression remains.
- AC3: Both modes retain S-136 stale safety: cancelled A callbacks cannot submit, reopen, clear or hide B; old release cannot dismiss a handed-off normal hive. Release then fresh drag works once: choose mode submits on drop; drop-first hands off once, submits only after choice. Preserve bounded pointer-probe recovery, defaults/settings, non-consuming binds, unrelated shortcuts and S-139 first-Escape Back.
- AC4: Evidence exercises production generated bindings, native Escape/surface Close and daemon callback/queue wiring, not only gate helpers. Pin revisions/tree hashes and CLI/daemon SHA256 before/after builds; cancellation creates zero outputs, recovery one decoded output, source inode/hash unchanged. Scoped gates pass; add one bilingual changelog entry and necessary bilingual binding-doc corrections only.
