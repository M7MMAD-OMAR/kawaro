    Use the hive

    • Open it: select files, right click, choose Kawaro. On Linux you can also hold the drop shortcut while dragging (shortcuts).
    • Read it: the centre cell shows the full name of the cell under your pointer.
    • Pick: click a cell. Cells that do not fit your selection are not drawn.
    • Result: saved next to the original under a new name, or in a subfolder if you change output in settings.
    • Progress: in the tray tooltip. On Windows, the tray menu has Cancel all.
    • Many files: actions that combine files, such as merging PDFs, appear when you select two or more.

    On Hyprland you can bind Super plus right click to open the hive. See the Hyprland guide.
